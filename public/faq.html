QUESTIONS? WE'VE GOT ANSWERS

Good to know before we clean.

Everything you need to feel comfortable before booking.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Estimates are free. The minimum service charge is $120, with final pricing based on the property size, condition, and work requested.

Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?

No. We normally bring our own supplies, but can use yours if you prefer.

Do you have pet-safe product options?

Alternative pet-safe cleaning products are available upon request. Let us know about pets and sensitivities before service.

Do you clean offices and vacation rentals?

Yes, these services were included in the business intake. Contact us to confirm scope and availability.

What areas do you serve?

Colorado Springs and nearby communities, including Fountain, Monument, Black Forest, and Pueblo, subject to travel availability.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, Cash App, Zelle, and Venmo.

Can I request a weekend or same-day appointment?

Weekend and same-day service may be available. Appointment requests are subject to confirmation.

Do you provide pest control or mitigation cleaning?

No. We do not offer pest control or mitigation cleaning.

Are all inquiries for professional cleaning only?

Yes. Please keep all inquiries respectful and related to professional cleaning services.

What is your cancellation or satisfaction policy?

A $50 cancellation fee was indicated in the intake, but the required notice period and satisfaction policy are being finalized. Please ask for the current terms before booking.
