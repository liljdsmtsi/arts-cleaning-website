What can a deep clean involve?

These are examples of common deep-cleaning tasks, not a confirmed checklist for every booking. Ali will confirm the exact scope during your free estimate.

Kitchen deep cleaning

A more detailed kitchen clean focused on grease and buildup around the backsplash, countertops, fixtures, cabinet fronts, appliance surfaces, edges, and hard-to-reach spots. Interior oven, refrigerator, and cabinet cleaning can be discussed and included in your quote when requested.

Bathroom detail cleaning

Sinks, fixtures, showers, tubs, and visible buildup.

Living areas and surfaces

Baseboards, reachable edges, doors, switches, and frequently touched surfaces.

Floors and finishing touches

Vacuuming, mopping, and detail work appropriate for the flooring.

TAILORED TO YOUR HOME

What's different from routine cleaning?

Routine cleaning helps maintain a space. Deep cleaning generally puts extra attention on buildup, edges, fixtures, and neglected surfaces.

  • Tell us which rooms need extra attention
  • Share photos or describe buildup and problem spots
  • Ask about inside-oven, inside-fridge, windows, and other extras before booking
  • Get a quote based on the size and condition of your space

Deep cleaning does not include pest control or mitigation cleaning. Specialty tasks and access requirements must be agreed on in advance.

Frequently asked questions

A few practical things worth knowing before your appointment.

Do I need to be home during the appointment?

You can discuss access arrangements when confirming your appointment. Ask Ali what she needs before the visit.

Should I put away clutter before the cleaners arrive?

Clearing personal items and fragile valuables can help the cleaning team reach more surfaces. Ask if you need help with a particular area.

Can I prioritize specific rooms or problem areas?

Yes, describe your priorities when requesting a quote so Ali can discuss the scope with you.

What should I do with pets during the visit?

Let Ali know about pets in advance and arrange a safe, comfortable space for them while cleaning is underway.

Can I switch to recurring cleaning afterward?

Recurring cleaning is available. Ask about a schedule that fits your home.
