Frequently asked questions

A few practical things worth knowing before your appointment.

Does the home need to be empty?

Let Ali know whether furniture or belongings will remain so the work can be scoped accurately.

Can cleaning be scheduled around my move date?

Include your move-out or move-in deadline when requesting an appointment. Timing is subject to availability.

Will this guarantee my security deposit?

No cleaning service can guarantee a landlord’s deposit decision. Share any move-out checklist from your property manager in advance.

Can I send pictures before the estimate?

Yes, describe the property and provide photos when the request process supports attachments; otherwise ask Ali how to send them.
