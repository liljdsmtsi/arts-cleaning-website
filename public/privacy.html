Privacy Policy

How A.R.T.S. Cleaning & Services LLC handles information you share with us.

Effective date

November 18, 2026

Who we are

A.R.T.S. Cleaning & Services LLC provides cleaning services in Colorado Springs and surrounding communities. For questions about this policy, contact artscleaningservicesllc@outlook.com or (719) 931-7870.

Information we collect

When you request an estimate, we collect the information you choose to provide, which may include your name, phone number, email address, city or ZIP code, requested cleaning service, preferred date, bedroom and bathroom counts, and notes about the property. If you contact us by phone, text, or email, we receive the information you share in those communications.

How we use your information

We use your information to respond to inquiries, prepare estimates, coordinate appointments, provide requested services, and maintain business records. We do not use an estimate request as permission to send unrelated promotional text messages.

How your request is processed

Our website uses Cloudflare to serve the site and process estimate requests. Submitted estimate information is saved in a Cloudflare D1 database. The website is configured to send request notifications through Resend email and, when enabled, Twilio text messaging. Information included in notifications is handled by those providers as part of delivering the message. Their own privacy practices also apply.

Cookies and technical information

Our estimate form does not require an advertising cookie. Like other hosting providers, Cloudflare may process technical information such as IP addresses and request metadata to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the website. We have not represented that all third-party analytics or cookies are disabled; this policy will be updated if additional tracking tools are introduced.

How long information is kept

Estimate requests and related correspondence may be retained for customer service and business recordkeeping. A fixed deletion schedule has not been established in this policy. You can contact us to request deletion of information that is no longer needed, subject to applicable legal or business recordkeeping requirements.

Sharing and protection

We share information with service providers as needed to operate the website and deliver communications, and when legally required. We do not sell customer information. We take reasonable steps to limit access to customer requests, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and requests

You may ask what information we hold about you, request a correction, or ask us to delete information where appropriate. Contact artscleaningservicesllc@outlook.com. We may need to verify your request before acting.

Updates to this policy

We may update this policy when our website, service providers, or data practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page.
